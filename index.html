RED DOCKET STUDIO

CASE FILES · TRUE CRIME DOCUMENTARIES

Red Docket is a YouTube channel that tells the stories of real, closed criminal cases: how they happened, how they were investigated and how they ended in court.

Red Docket Studio is the private production tool used by the channel's own team. It runs on our own computer and does two things with Google services:

  • uploads the channel's finished videos to its own YouTube account as private drafts, so they can be reviewed before publishing;
  • sends a notification email to the channel owner when a new video is ready for review.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
