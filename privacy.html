Privacy Policy

Red Docket Studio · Effective 9 November 2026

Red Docket Studio ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the operator of the Red Docket YouTube channel to manage that channel's own videos. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

With the account owner's consent through Google's sign-in screen, the app requests these permissions:

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails as private videos.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to show the connected channel's name and confirm uploads.
  • Gmail send (gmail.send): to send a notification email from the owner's account to the owner's own address when a video is ready. The app cannot read, search or delete emails.

How the data is used and stored

  • The app runs only on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • YouTube uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local storage folder removes all stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Red Docket YouTube channel's About page.
